# Pre-submission email to Computer Science Review

**Send before** any cold submission. Per the journal's Guide for Authors, CSR is invitation-leaning:

> *"Authors should provide a PDF or PS copy of their manuscript to the Editor who invited the author to write the survey."*

> *"At least one author is expected to have at least three papers on the subject of the survey published in high impact factor journals or highly ranked conferences and listed in the bibliographic references of your submission."*

## Suggested email template

> Subject: Survey fit inquiry — *A Survey of Reinforcement Learning for Economics*
>
> Dear Professor Kratochvíl,
>
> I am preparing a survey on reinforcement learning for economics under the supervision of John Rust at Georgetown University. Drawing on the master draft (~138 pp on arXiv) and a thematic compression (~30 pp), I would like to confirm that an unsolicited submission is welcome and that the topic falls within *Computer Science Review*'s scope before submitting through Editorial Manager.
>
> The survey covers reinforcement learning algorithms, multi-agent learning and algorithmic collusion, off-policy evaluation, RLHF and human-feedback alignment, and economic bandits. The 30-pp Computer Science Review version would foreground the cross-disciplinary taxonomy and the simulation-supported empirical evaluations.
>
> I would be grateful for any guidance on whether this fits the journal's editorial direction. A 1-pp summary is attached.
>
> Sincerely,
> Pranjal Rawat
