## Status under the brief's filters

AI Magazine is **not strictly survey-only** (it's a mixed magazine). Per the memo, it's a fall-back rather than a primary target. Listed for honesty in the strategy because cold submissions are welcome and surveys are explicitly accepted.

## Framing

Practitioner / policymaker-facing. Per `journal_target.md` Recommendations §6:
> *"AI Magazine for a shorter (~10-15 page) practitioner-facing version with the simulation code as the central artefact. Not survey-only per the brief's strict definition, but openly accepts unsolicited surveys, OA, no APC for the author."*

Lead with one motivating example (algorithmic collusion is the most policy-resonant topic for a 2026 readership — Calvano et al. 2020 *AER* is the anchor citation), then a brief tour of bandits, RLHF, and causal RL. Foreground the simulation repository as the central artefact: link prominently, point to specific scripts that practitioners can run.
