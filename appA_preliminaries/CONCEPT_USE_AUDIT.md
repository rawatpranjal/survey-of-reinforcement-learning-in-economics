# Appendix A concept-to-use audit

The inclusion rule is direct use in the compiled survey. The complete term-by-term control surface is `TERM_LEDGER.md`.

| Appendix block | Direct survey use |
|---|---|
| Compact RL-theory reference | Fixes the vocabulary used across Chapters 2 through 13, including Bellman residuals, bootstrapping, regret, coverage, orthogonality, risk, robustness, and world models. |
| Weighted operator geometry | Supports FQI, projected Bellman operators, the deadly triad, natural gradients, and TRPO in Chapters 2 and 3. |
| Occupancy and coverage | Supports concentrability bounds in Chapter 3, offline RL in Chapter 8, causal OPE in Chapters 10 and 10b, and the field OPE comparison in Chapter 13. |
| Regret and sample complexity | Supports the theory rates in Chapter 3 and the pricing and game regret analyses in Chapters 6 and 7. |
| Orthogonal scores | Supports doubly robust OPE, dynamic DML, and policy learning in Chapters 10 and 10b. |
| Risk and robust operators | Supports quantile, CVaR, distributionally robust, and constrained RL in Chapter 11. |
| Spectral, probabilistic, convex, and fixed-point results | Preserves the theorem-level machinery invoked throughout the theory and application chapters. |

Elementary matrix multiplication, unit-ball diagrams, coordinate examples, and other undergraduate review material were removed. Determinants, singular-value decomposition, pseudoinverses, tensors, and quotient spaces remain excluded because the compiled survey does not rely on them.
