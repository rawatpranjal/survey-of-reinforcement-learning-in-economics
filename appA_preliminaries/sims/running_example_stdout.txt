A.7  STOCHASTIC APPROXIMATION: TABULAR TD(0) UNDER FOUR STEP SIZES
======================================================================
  Evaluating pi* = (keep, replace) from sampled transitions, 20 seeds,
  20000 steps each, target V^pi = [good 5.3448, worn 4.3103].

                schedule       sum a_t     sum a_t^2   final sup error      s.e.
                constant       1000.00       50.0000            0.1711    0.0220
              one_over_n         19.46        3.2896            1.7125    0.0235
      one_over_n_pow_0.7         98.82        6.0813            0.0450    0.0057
      one_over_n_squared          3.29        2.1646            4.0291    0.0229

  Reading the two Robbins-Monro conditions off the two sums, where n counts
  visits to the state being updated:
    constant   : sum a_n diverges, which is what the first condition asks, but
                 sum a_n^2 diverges too, so the noise is never damped and the
                 iterate settles into a floor set by the step size.
    1/n^2      : sum a_n^2 converges, but sum a_n converges as well, so the
                 total movement available is finite and the iterate stalls
                 short of the target no matter how long it runs.
    1/n        : both conditions hold, so this converges, but only in the
                 limit. Its total movement grows like log n, so after 20000
                 steps it is still further from the target than the constant
                 step, which converges fast and then stops improving. The
                 conditions are asymptotic and say nothing about the rate.
    1/n^0.7    : both conditions also hold, and the total movement grows like
                 n^0.3 instead of log n, which is why the polynomial family is
                 what reinforcement learning actually uses.

  Ranked by final sup-norm error at this budget:
      one_over_n_pow_0.7  0.0450  (s.e. 0.0057)
                constant  0.1711  (s.e. 0.0220)
              one_over_n  1.7125  (s.e. 0.0235)
      one_over_n_squared  4.0291  (s.e. 0.0229)
  At 20000 steps the smallest error is one_over_n_pow_0.7. Both Robbins-Monro
  conditions are necessary for convergence and neither is sufficient for speed.
